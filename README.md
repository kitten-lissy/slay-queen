# Slay Queen

Shows what to bring for your current slayer task, using the gear you actually own.

## Features

- **Best gear you own** for each slot, picked from your bank, inventory and equipment using real item stats and the monster's weakness
- **Best weapon and style by damage**: compares the weapons you own (for example whip, tentacle and Osmumten's fang) against the task monster's defences from the OSRS Wiki, and on Auto picks the style that hits hardest with your gear
- **Task-specific gear** first where it matters: salve amulet for undead, dragonhunter gear, leaf-bladed weapons, demonbane, keris, and more
- **Required items** (rock hammer, bag of salt, earmuffs, nose peg, mirror shield, ice coolers...) marked as owned or missing
- **Supplies** to bring (prayer potions or food, antifire, cannonballs, runes)
- **Cannon** rules for the task (yes / no / some areas)
- **Burst/barrage tasks**: stack location, and an Ancient Magicks (or chinchompa) loadout when "Use AoE when viable" is on
- **Loot helpers** worth bringing: herb sack, seed box, gem bag, looting bag, bonecrusher, ash sanctifier
- **Highlighting** of everything recommended in the bank and on worn equipment (and optionally the inventory), plus a `tag:slayqueen` bank search that shows only those items

## Usage

Get or check a slayer task, then open your bank. The overlay shows whenever you have a task (can be limited to when the bank is open). The pink heart side panel has the full details. Open your bank once so the plugin knows what you own.

## Notes

- Task advice is compiled from the Old School RuneScape Wiki.
- Item level requirements aren't available to plugins, so a suggested item may be one you can't equip yet.
- Boss tasks show gear and items only, not boss mechanics.
- Damage estimates use your real (unboosted) levels and assume no prayers or potions.
